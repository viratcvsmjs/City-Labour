Privacy Policy

Company Name: City Labour | Last Updated: July 03, 2026

At City Labour, we value your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal data of our users—both workers ("City-Makers") and employers. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application or website.

By using our services, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

1. Information We Collect

To provide a functional, safe, and secure marketplace for daily wage and skilled labor, we collect the following types of information:

  • Identity Information: Full name, mobile phone number, age, and profile picture.
  • Government Identification: We may collect government-issued ID details (such as Aadhaar, Voter ID, or labor cards) to verify workers for safety, trust, and transparency.
  • Professional Details: Trade skills (e.g., electrician, plumber, mason), daily wage expectations, and availability.
  • Location Data: We collect precise location data to connect workers with nearby employment opportunities. This data may be collected in the background to ensure real-time job matching.
  • Media Access: Permission to access your camera or gallery to allow you to upload a profile picture or verify identification documents.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected data for the following purposes:

  • Identity Verification: To ensure the authenticity of all users on the platform and prevent fraud.
  • Job Matching: To connect workers with relevant job openings and employers in their immediate geographic vicinity.
  • Communication: To allow employers to directly contact workers for potential hiring and project discussions.
  • Service Improvement: To analyze app usage, fix bugs, and enhance user experience.

3. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We respect your privacy and do not sell your personal data to third-party marketers. Sharing occurs only under the following strict circumstances:

  • With Employers/Contractors: A worker’s name, skills, location, and contact number are shared with registered employers to facilitate hiring.
  • For Legal Reasons: We may disclose your information if required to do so by law or in response to valid requests by public authorities (e.g., a court or government agency).

4. Data Security

We implement robust technical and organizational security measures to protect your data from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. All communication channels and sensitive data transfers within the portal are secured using encryption protocols (SSL/TLS).

5. Data Deletion and User Rights

In compliance with application marketplace standards, users have full control over their account data.

  • Right to Delete: You can request the permanent deletion of your account and all associated personal data at any time.

How to Request Deletion: Click the "Request Data Deletion" button below or email us directly at support@citylabour.com with the subject line "Data Deletion Request".

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date at the top.
